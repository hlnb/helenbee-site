## Core Angle

This piece is about low-demand connection after service: the quiet value of being expected somewhere, even when nothing deep is being processed.

Use gentle, grounded visuals: coffee cups, a small cafe table, hands around mugs, walking toward a familiar place, an empty chair becoming occupied, ordinary local scenes.

Visual direction:
- Close shot of a coffee cup arriving at a table
- Empty chair

- Slow pan across a cafe corner

On-screen text:

There is a table I go back to every week.

Same corner.

I have changed my mind.

For a long time, I thought connection had to be deep to count.

The Table I Know I'll Be At is published on 25 May.

## Instagram Reel 3: After Service

Length: 30-40 seconds

Visual direction:
- Quiet morning coffee
- A street or cafe exterior
- A chair at a small table
- Cut to the article title on screen

Voiceover:

```text
When I left the RAAF, I lost something I did not have a name for at the time.

It was not just structure.
It was not just role.

It was belonging.

The kind that came built into service life.

Civilian life does not come with that.

So I have had to learn how belonging can be rebuilt in smaller, quieter ways.
```

Caption:

```text
Leaving service can mean losing more than a job, a structure, or a role.

Sometimes what disappears is the built-in belonging: the shared language, the familiar rules, the sense of being understood without having to explain the whole context.

This piece sits with what it means to rebuild connection after that.

Not perfectly. Not dramatically.

Just one ordinary table at a time.

#Veterans #RAAF #VeteranMentalHealth #Belonging #Loneliness #ConnectionMatters #LifeAfterService
```

## Feed Post 3: Research Thread Without Losing Voice

Image idea: coffee table, notebook, or a quiet cafe corner.

```text
There is research for the kind of connection I resisted for years.

The familiar face.
The person at the cafe.
The group where you are known enough to be noticed, but not required to explain everything.

Researchers call these weak ties or fringeships. There is even a phrase I keep coming back to: Vitamin S.

Social contact as a nutrient.

Not always a feast.
Not always something deep and transformative.
Sometimes just a small dose of ordinary human contact, taken regularly enough to make a difference.

That is what this article is about.

#VitaminS #WeakTies #Belonging #SocialConnection #VeteranMentalHealth #Community #Recovery
```
